## 🃏 Game Mechanics
- How many cards are in the deck? (8 pairs? 16 pairs?)
- Do cards need to match exactly, or by category?
- How many cards can the player flip at once? (typically 2)
- What happens when a match is found? (stay face-up, disappear, glow?)
- What happens when cards don't match? (flip back after a delay — how long?)
- Can the player flip a third card while two are already revealed?
- Is there a move limit or time limit?
- Does the game have difficulty levels?

---

## 🎨 Card Design
- What goes on the card faces? (emojis, images, numbers, letters, icons?)
- What does the card back look like? (solid color, pattern, image?)
- What size are the cards?
- Is there a flip animation? (CSS 3D transform?)
- What does a matched card look like vs. an unmatched one?

---

## 🗂️ Grid & Layout
- What's the grid layout? (4x4, 4x3, 6x6?)
- Is the grid responsive (mobile-friendly)?
- Is the grid fixed or does it change based on difficulty?

---

## 🏆 Scoring & Progression
- Is there a score? What does it track? (matches, moves, time, or a combo?)
- Is there a timer counting up or counting down?
- Are there penalties for mismatches?
- Is there a high score / leaderboard?
- Are high scores saved? (localStorage?)
- Is there more than one level?

---

## 🔀 Shuffling
- How are cards shuffled? (Fisher-Yates algorithm?)
- Are cards reshuffled on every new game?
- Is the shuffle seeded (same layout every time) or random?

---

## 🎮 Game States
- What is the initial/start state? (splash screen, or straight to board?)
- What happens when the game is won?
- Is there a "Game Over" state (if timer runs out)?
- Can the player pause the game?
- Is there a restart button? Does it reshuffle?

---

## 🔊 Audio & Feedback
- Is there sound? (card flip, match, mismatch, win?)
- Is sound on by default or off?
- Is there visual feedback beyond card state? (screen flash, confetti?)

---

## 💾 State Management
- Where does game state live? (JS object, class, or just loose variables?)
- What needs to be tracked at any time? (flipped cards, matched pairs, move count, timer)
- Do you save progress to localStorage?

---

## 🛠️ Code Architecture
- Is this all in one file or split into HTML/CSS/JS files?
- Are you using classes, modules, or plain functions?
- How do you represent a card in JS? (object with id, value, isFlipped, isMatched?)
- How do you prevent the player from clicking during the "check" delay?
- How do you dynamically generate the board in JS vs. hardcoding HTML?

---

## 📱 UX & Accessibility
- Does it work on mobile/touch screens?
- Is it keyboard navigable?
- Are there ARIA labels for screen readers?
- What font and color scheme are you using?
- Is there a dark/light mode?

---

## 🃏 `Card` Class
- What properties does a Card have?
   - `id`? `value`? `isFlipped`? `isMatched`?
- Does the Card know about its **DOM element**, or is that handled externally?
- Does the Card have methods like `flip()`, `match()`, `reset()`?
- Does `flip()` toggle state only, or does it also update the DOM?
- Does a Card know how to **render itself**, or does something else render it?
- Is Card responsible for its own **click event**, or does the Board/Controller handle that?

---

## 🗂️ `Board` Class
- What does the Board own? (the grid element, the array of Cards?)
- Is the Board responsible for **generating and shuffling** cards, or does it receive them?
- Does the Board render the grid to the DOM, or just hold state?
- Does the Board have methods like `shuffle()`, `render()`, `reset()`?
- Does the Board know about **matches** — or is that the Controller's job?
- Does the Board expose a way to get cards? (`getCards()`, `getUnmatched()`?)
- Does the Board listen for click events, or does it delegate to the Controller?

---

## 🎮 `GameController` Class
- What does the Controller orchestrate? (Board, ScoreTracker, Timer, all of the above?)
- Where does the **core game logic** live — matching, turn validation, win condition?
- Does the Controller hold the two currently flipped cards?
- Does `onCardClick(card)` live here?
- Who calls `startGame()`, `endGame()`, `resetGame()` — the Controller or something external?
- Does the Controller talk to the Board directly, or through an interface?
- Does the Controller update the UI (score, timer display), or is that a separate class?

---

## 📋 Interfaces
- Do you have a `ICard` interface? What's on it?
- Do you have a `IBoard` interface — useful if you want swappable board types (4x4, 6x6)?
- Do you have a `IGameController` interface?
- Do you have a separate `IScoreTracker` or `ITimer` interface?
- Do you want a `IRenderer` interface so DOM logic is swappable/testable?
- Do you want a `IEventEmitter` / observer pattern so classes don't directly depend on each other?

---

## 🔗 Relationships & Dependencies
- Does `Card` depend on `Board`? (it probably shouldn't)
- Does `Board` depend on `GameController`? (probably not — Controller should depend on Board)
- Who creates the Cards — the Board or the Controller?
- Do you use **dependency injection** (pass Board into Controller) or does Controller instantiate Board itself?
- Is there a top-level `Game` or `App` class that wires everything together?

---

## 🧩 Supporting Classes
- Do you have a `Deck` class separate from `Board`? (generates & shuffles cards)
- Do you have a `Timer` class with `start()`, `stop()`, `reset()`, `onTick()`?
- Do you have a `ScoreTracker` class with `addMove()`, `addMatch()`, `getScore()`?
- Do you have a `UIRenderer` or `DOMManager` class that handles all DOM updates?
- Do you have an `EventBus` / `EventEmitter` for loose coupling between classes?

---

## 🧠 State Ownership
- Which class owns **game phase**? (idle, playing, paused, won, lost)
- Which class owns **current flipped cards**?
- Which class owns **move count** and **match count**?
- Which class owns **the timer value**?
- Is there a single `GameState` object passed around, or is state distributed across classes?
